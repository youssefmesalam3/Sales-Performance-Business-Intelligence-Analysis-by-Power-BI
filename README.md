# 📊 Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Analysis

An interactive **Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, products, regions, customers, and order trends.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through **Power Query, DAX, and Data Modeling**, providing an interactive dashboard that supports data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Analysis** project focuses on analyzing sales data across multiple years, regions, products, and customer types.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Profit and profit margin
- Orders and quantity sold
- Regional performance
- Product performance
- Customer analysis
- Product status
- Customer types
- Monthly and yearly trends

The dashboard is designed to help businesses understand their overall performance and identify the products, regions, and customers contributing most to revenue and profitability.

## 🔄 Data Analysis Workflow

The project follows a complete Business Intelligence workflow:

### 1. Data Preparation

Raw datasets were imported into Power BI and prepared for analysis.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Transformation — Power Query

**Power Query** was used to:

- Clean and transform the raw datasets
- Prepare tables for analysis
- Standardize and organize data
- Create required calculated fields
- Prepare the sales data for the final data model

### 3. Data Modeling

A structured **Data Model** was created in Power BI to establish relationships between the main datasets, including:

- Sales
- Customers
- Products
- Regions

This allowed the different dimensions to interact correctly with the sales data and enabled dynamic dashboard analysis.

### 4. DAX

**DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** was used to create analytical measures and KPIs for evaluating:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Customers
- Total Products
- Active Products
- Product and customer rankings
- Yearly and regional performance

### 5. Dashboard Development

The final Power BI dashboard was developed using interactive visuals, KPI cards, charts, slicers, and analytical views.
